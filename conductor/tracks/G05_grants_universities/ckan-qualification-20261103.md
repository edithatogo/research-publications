# Australian grants catalogue qualification, 3 November 2026

Both public CKAN package_search routes returned HTTP 200 and successful JSON without credentials.
The search for grants returned 588 catalogue records in Queensland and 3,322 nationally.
The first 100 results per catalogue were inspected as a bounded discovery sample.
A quoted search for "grant opportunities" returned zero and six respectively.
These are dataset counts. The national catalogue also returned Queensland resources,
including the same Grants Finder CSV, so a second catalogue listing adds no coverage.

[Queensland API](https://www.data.qld.gov.au/api/3/action/package_search?q=grants&rows=1)
and [Commonwealth API](https://data.gov.au/data/api/3/action/package_search?q=grants&rows=1).

The Queensland Grants Finder resource describes contributions. The 2026 small business
grants package lists successful recipients. The national search surfaced an Adelaide
package describing endorsed applications. These packages do not supply current calls.

The grants-program-summary package identifies the issuing council and points readers to
its current programs. Following that lead, Brisbane's Community Celebration Donation
accepts applications from 27 October through 17 November 2026 for eligible local groups.
This council round demonstrates discovery outside federal funding. The issuing page
supplies the application dates. It is a community donation and has no established
research fit or CHHHS eligibility.

[Council program metadata](https://www.data.qld.gov.au/dataset/grants-program-summary)
and [issuing council round details](https://www.brisbane.qld.gov.au/community-support-and-safety/grants-and-sponsorship/applying-for-a-grant/community-grants).

Use both APIs for catalogue discovery. No sampled resource qualified as an automated
feed of current research grant rounds. Before admitting a round, verify its issuing
record, open dates, eligibility and reuse, then compare its issuer and round identity
against the existing register. No new opportunity was admitted in this assessment.
